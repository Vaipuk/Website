# Build a cinematic entrance from continuity

**Replace the basic title-and-shutter reveal with a finite 3,600ms construction-to-camera sequence:** copper points assemble a VB/lens-like geometry, existing photographs unstack into depth, the oversized Fraunces name resolves, and the whole composition passes through an exposure change into the requested page. The strongest references are Igloo Inc’s construction of a world and Active Theory’s dramatic change of scale, supported by Codrops’ concrete image-grid, trail, and kinetic-type mechanics. Their lesson is continuity: each visual state should cause the next, instead of adding more decoration around a title. A bounded Canvas 2D layer, eight CSS 3D photographic planes, and native animation are sufficient for this adaptation; copying their WebGL engines is unnecessary. **Replay on every fresh document open and refresh, on every route**, while retaining immediate Skip/Escape and reduced-motion bypasses. The timings and composition below are an original recommendation, not a measured or copied sequence from any reference.

## Igloo and Active Theory make the opening a spatial event

**Observed in the research session on 12 November 2026:** Igloo begins with a flat blue-grey field and a tiny animated white mark, then shows its igloo and terrain crossed by bright triangulated scan-lines, before the scene resolves into textured ice, mountains, a luminous core, and atmospheric depth. The useful event is construction followed by material resolution; the subject survives into the usable site. In the creator’s account, abeto explains that an ordinary outdoor beginning did not establish the desired tone, so the team made a technical introduction in real time and connected it to the rest of the experience. This is stronger guidance than a fashionable loader treatment: the opening establishes what the world is and how to read it. ([Igloo live site](https://www.igloo.inc/), [abeto’s creator case study](https://www.awwwards.com/igloo-inc-case-study.html))

**Observed endpoints, rather than a recorded camera path:** Active Theory opens black with a small cyan indicator; a later state contains an enormous, almost black embossed circular/angular mark; its settled view contains a much smaller medallion suspended in a dark volume with colored specks and long light strands. The useful grammar is the movement between intimate surface detail and a wide spatial view. The cofounders describe WebGL/JavaScript, simple work/contact navigation, and extensive polish, but those implementation choices are not a requirement for borrowing scale and depth. The older Active Theory v5 journey through office-inspired environments belongs to another version and should not be presented as the current opening. ([Active Theory live site](https://activetheory.net/), [current creator interview](https://www.commarts.com/webpicks/active-theory-2), [historical v5 interview](https://www.webbyawards.com/crafted-with-code/active-theory/))

**Design interpretation:** combine Igloo’s progression from structure to substance with Active Theory’s near/far change, using the portfolio’s own photographs and identity. The rejected dark copper/Fraunces treatment already had a coherent palette, but an elegant title, line, shutter, and fade do not give the viewer a consequential visual event. A copper diagram becoming a spatial photographic composition does. Resn’s faceted gem supports the idea of a central object acting as a threshold, but its click-and-hold invitation would introduce an unnecessary gate here. Lusion was observed with an elaborate percentage loader and a separate live hero, but the exact connection between them was not captured; it is a weaker reference for the continuity this replacement needs. ([Resn live site](https://resn.co.nz/), [Lusion live site](https://lusion.co/))

## Codrops supplies concrete mechanics behind the spectacle

Codrops’ **Grid Flow Animation** describes thumbnails migrating with stagger into navigation and expanding back into content-grid positions. **Image Trail Animation for an Intro** describes one photograph moving between successive layouts while leaving a trail, followed by an Enter-driven rearrangement. These establish two transferable mechanics: coordinate images as one composition, and retain an identifiable subject as its spatial state changes. They do not justify fake progress, an Enter gate, or an endless cursor trail. The proposed photographic prints should share a direction and visual center rather than rotate independently as random cards. ([Grid Flow creator article](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2023/07/27/grid-flow-animation/), [Image Trail creator article](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2022/05/03/image-trail-animation-for-an-intro/))

The original **Kinetic Typography Page Transition** source makes the typography itself a moving spatial field: its container scales to 2.7 and rotates −90 degrees, while staggered lines travel across and out of view. Its companion source starts the new content before the type has finished, using opposite image/wrapper offsets for a masked reveal. The important implementation lesson is **overlap the destination with the departing composition**; waiting for every effect to finish before revealing a second page entrance creates dead air. This portfolio should adapt monumental scale, controlled line movement, and overlap without reproducing the demo’s exact rotation, palette, or repeated copy. ([Original type-transition code](https://github.com/codrops/KineticTypePageTransition/blob/main/src/js/typeTransition.js), [original content-reveal code](https://github.com/codrops/KineticTypePageTransition/blob/main/src/js/index.js))

The particle and depth choices also have direct precedents. Codrops’ **Interactive Particles Slideshow** demonstrates Canvas-built text and shapes; its later **3D Typing Effects** tutorial starts by sampling text on Canvas for particle coordinates. **On-Scroll Perspective Grid Animations** demonstrates coordinated image planes in perspective, while **Image To Grid Transition** moves a large image into a grid slot. The recommendation is to adapt those primitives into a short autonomous opening, not to copy their scroll behavior or dependencies. “Contact sheet,” the VB/lens geometry, and the timing below are this portfolio’s proposed composition. Some Codrops article opens returned HTTP 403 during research; the notes used indexed creator text, available original demos, and the linked source files. No unviewed intermediate motion is claimed as an observation. ([Particle demo](https://tympanus.net/Development/InteractiveParticlesSlideshow/), [Canvas sampling tutorial](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2022/11/08/3d-typing-effects-with-three-js/), [perspective-grid demo](https://tympanus.net/Development/Scroll3DGrid/), [image-to-grid demo](https://tympanus.net/Development/ImageToGridTransition/))

## One 3,600ms composition gives every layer a purpose

**Proposed choreography, 0–650ms:** the first visible frame already contains copper points and a sharply cropped photographic print against the existing dark background. The points converge into one legible VB/lens-like structure; sparse connecting strokes make the geometry feel measured rather than glittery. This is the construction beat. Begin the photo unstack around 200ms so the canvas and photographs belong to one event. Avoid a blank hold, boot text, loading percentage, or newly invented slogan.

**Proposed choreography, 650–1,650ms:** eight existing photographic prints unfold from the same center into deliberate depth planes. Use coordinated lateral travel, modest roll, and large near/far differences; one or two close passes supply the camera sensation. The copper structure loosens and disperses through the same space as the oversized Fraunces name moves into the central negative area. Let the name become fully legible around 1,400ms. The particle geometry is an opening question, the images supply the personal subject, and the name resolves identity; none needs to repeat the job of the other.

**Proposed choreography, 1,650–2,450ms:** hold the readable name while the photographs briefly settle into an asymmetric composed arrangement. Keep only subtle residual drift. This pause is essential: constant acceleration would turn the sequence into an indistinct effects reel. Then move the image group forward and outward as one camera passage; the central copper geometry disperses instead of becoming a separate logo sting. The name should resolve once, without scrambling letters or adding branding copy.

**Proposed choreography, 2,450–3,600ms:** expose the already-rendered requested route while the last photographic planes pass the viewer. A restrained change of exposure and scale connects the dark composition to the page; do not close an iris or shutter and reopen it. Make most destination content visible before the final plane exits, and finish with no ambient overlay or second compulsory entrance. Because replay applies to every route, **the ending must reveal any underlying page without requiring a particular homepage hero image or layout**. That is a deliberate tradeoff: it gives up a perfect image-to-hero match in exchange for a coherent opening on deep links, blog pages, and other routes.

## Bound the rendering and make every exit complete

**Implementation recommendation:** use one decorative Canvas 2D layer for the copper field, semantic DOM for the readable name and Skip control, and native keyframed transforms for the photographic planes. Keep one elapsed-time origin and one 3,600ms lifecycle deadline. Canvas work should use timestamp-based requestAnimationFrame, precomputed targets, and fixed particle arrays; avoid per-frame React updates, text sampling, allocation, layout reads, shadow blur, and large image filters. Initial ceilings of **1,600 points on desktop, 700 on phones, and a 1.5× backing-resolution cap** are starting budgets, not proven frame-rate guarantees. Drop to four to six photographic planes and shorter depth travel on narrow screens while retaining the same sequence. ([Canvas optimization guidance](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Canvas_API/Tutorial/Optimizing_canvas), [requestAnimationFrame contract](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Window/requestAnimationFrame))

Reuse compressed local thumbnails rather than loading full-resolution originals; existing images can repeat across eight prints. Keep perspective and preserve-3d on an inner scene, and apply cropping or exposure treatment to a separate wrapper. Opacity, filters, and other grouping properties can flatten a preserving 3D scene, so fade individual leaves where necessary. Animate mostly transforms and restrained opacity, applying will-change only for the finite opening. Native transforms offer a compositor opportunity; they do not eliminate decode, raster, layer-memory, or overdraw costs. **No external assets, video, new animation package, or rendering engine are necessary.** ([Web Animations API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Element/animate), [3D flattening rules](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Properties/transform-style), [will-change memory guidance](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Properties/will-change), [Chromium rendering architecture](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/chromium/renderingng-architecture))

Replay must be document-scoped: remove sessionStorage suppression and homepage-only eligibility, mount the opening in the shared application shell, and allow fresh loads and refreshes of all routes, including deliberate deep links. Ordinary client-side navigation should not restart it; navigation during playback should finish the overlay immediately. These requirements supersede the earlier homepage-only suggestion in the technique notes. Preserve the existing Skip/Escape behavior and use a single idempotent finish path for automatic completion, explicit skip, route change, reduced-motion change, failure, and unmount. It must cancel Canvas frames, native Animation handles, timers, and listeners; restore the exact prior scroll state; and release dialog focus to an appropriate page location without overriding an anchor destination. Handle cancellation promises and ignore late font/decode work after disposal. ([Animation cancellation](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Animation/cancel), [image decoding](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/HTMLImageElement/decode))

**Reduced motion should bypass the choreography immediately**, not compress the same camera dive or impose a static 3.6-second wait. Make all graphical layers decorative and keep Skip visible and keyboard usable from the first frame. Neither font readiness nor image decode should gate the deadline: missing assets should degrade the scene or expose the page. Complete rather than replaying delayed motion after a background-tab return. Verification must cover fresh open and refresh on the home route and a deep route, internal navigation, Skip and Escape, focus/scroll restoration, initial and live reduced-motion preferences, image/font failure, narrow layouts, StrictMode cleanup, and absence of animation work after removal. Profile actual devices before claiming smoothness; the reference sites were not audited for accessibility, mobile performance, or asset budgets. ([Reduced-motion guidance](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/At-rules/@media/prefers-reduced-motion), [font readiness](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/FontFaceSet/ready), [background animation behavior](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Window/requestAnimationFrame))

## Conclusion

A photography-only contact-sheet passage is the simplest fallback if the hybrid becomes visually crowded; it preserves personal material and depth while removing the Canvas layer. A type-only spatial transition is lighter still, but less specific to the owner. Full WebGL construction or liquid displacement offers richer material behavior at the cost of a larger renderer and lifecycle surface; the available evidence does not justify that cost for a short repeated entrance. The hybrid earns its additional layer only if geometry, photographs, and name read as successive states of one composition. Judge the implementation by that continuity, legibility, and immediate escape—not by how many independent effects fit inside 3.6 seconds.
